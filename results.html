How Students Get These Results

Every store above went through the same four steps of the mentorship.

  1. Step 1: Plan

    Offer and signals

    Lock a clear offer and shortlist products with real demand, margin and velocity, then validate in a 72-hour loop before spending big.

  2. Step 2: Build

    Store and funnel

    Fix page speed, clarity and checkout friction in order, aiming for a store that loads in under 3 seconds.

  3. Step 3: Launch

    Creatives and ads

    Ship identity-driven hooks, cut fast and judge every ad against KPI gates instead of gut feel.

  4. Step 4: Scale

    Rules and guardrails

    Raise spend on rules: daily caps, ROAS floors and supplier sync, so growth doesn't bleed cash.

Questions About Student Results

Are these results real?

Yes. Every dashboard and message on this page was posted by a student in the ChimeRaid Ecom Discord, in the wins and student-wins channels. We use their screenshots as they shared them, with no mockups or stock images.

Are these typical results?

No. These are some of our strongest weeks, not a promise of what you will make. Your niche, ad budget and how fast you execute all change the outcome. Every student starts at the first order, like the #1001 screenshot above.

How much do I need to get started?

Plans start at $499 for 3 months. On top of that, expect an initial ad test budget of around $300 to $600 for the first 72-hour validation round. We only scale spend once the numbers hold.

How long until I see my first sale?

There is no fixed timeline. We validate products in 72 hours, then scale only when the numbers hold. Students who iterate creatives fast and keep clean analytics tend to move quicker.
